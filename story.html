The Idea

Built For The
Hour Between

Dawn Era isn't about waiting for good things to happen. It's for the ones already moving while the city's still quiet — three steps ahead by the time the sun's fully up.

The name comes from that specific hour — the one between night and morning, when the old day is already over and the new one hasn't been claimed by anyone yet. It's a blank page. Whoever's awake for it gets to decide what it becomes.

Every piece we make is built around that same idea: minimal enough to move in, bold enough to mean something. No noise, no filler — just the pieces that earn their place in a small, considered wardrobe.

This is the first era. Drop 01 is coming — built slow, on purpose, by one person who'd rather get it right than get it out fast.
